# ELogSync Personal v0.1.10

## 简体中文

### 下载与安装

- **Windows x64 安装器**：`ELogSync-Personal-v0.1.10-windows-x64-setup.exe`
- **SHA-256**：`403E2957A0C27593F715649B9A73D47FABE6F047795EF24806300FC81CE2D150`
- 安装器采用“当前用户”模式，不要求管理员权限。请先关闭正在运行的一录清，再运行安装器完成安装或升级。

### 本版内容

- 随手记点击“AI 整理”后改为在当前页面显示处理对话框；对处理中、成功、失败与超时给出明确反馈，成功后再进入待确认的业务信息页面。
- 英文界面补齐随手记、待推进、首页和商机中的固定系统文案，包括详情、候选确认、状态、提示、空态和自动生成的系统默认值。
- AI 连接检测与随手记整理的服务反馈会按当前界面语言显示；用户填写的业务内容和 AI 候选内容仍保留其原始语言。
- 商机详情中的未设置状态、关联业务卡片和相关提示在英文界面中使用英文固定文案。

### 数据与更新提醒

- **本机数据格式版本：76**。本版包含从数据格式 75 到 76 的一次升级；后续版本将按各自发布说明中的数据格式兼容性判断升级路径。
- 一录清个人版只在本机保存业务数据。升级前建议在“系统配置 → 备份与恢复”创建完整备份。
- 本次不改变业务字段、已有记录、附件或 AI 候选内容的原文。
- 本次仅提供 Windows x64 NSIS 安装器；MSI 和 macOS 安装器尚未发布。

## English

### Download and install

- **Windows x64 installer**: `ELogSync-Personal-v0.1.10-windows-x64-setup.exe`
- **SHA-256**: `403E2957A0C27593F715649B9A73D47FABE6F047795EF24806300FC81CE2D150`
- The installer is per-user and does not require administrator privileges. Close ELogSync before installing or updating.

### Highlights

- Selecting **AI Organization** from a Quick Note now shows a processing dialog in place. It reports progress, success, failure, and timeout before opening the business-information review page.
- Completed English fixed-copy coverage in Quick Notes, Follow-ups, Home, and Opportunities, including details, candidate review, statuses, guidance, empty states, and system-generated defaults.
- AI connection checks and Quick Note organization feedback now follow the active interface language. User-entered business content and AI candidates retain their original language.
- Opportunity detail pages now display unset states, related-business cards, and supporting guidance in English in the English interface.

### Data and update notes

- **Local data format version: 76.** This release includes one upgrade from data format 75 to 76. Later releases determine upgrade paths from the data-format compatibility stated in their release notes.
- ELogSync Personal stores business data locally. Create a full backup from **System Settings → Backup & Restore** before upgrading.
- This release does not change business fields, existing records, attachments, or the original language of AI candidates.
- This release provides only a Windows x64 NSIS installer. MSI and macOS installers are not published yet.
